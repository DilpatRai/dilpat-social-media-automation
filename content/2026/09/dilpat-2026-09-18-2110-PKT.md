## LinkedIn Caption
A reliable CI/CD pipeline is a system, not just a script.

Jenkins can coordinate a delivery flow such as:

- Checkout: retrieve the exact source revision
- Verify: run unit tests, static checks, and packaging validation
- Build: create a reproducible artifact or container image
- Publish: store the versioned output
- Deploy: promote the artifact to a target environment

The value comes from repeatability and feedback. When every commit follows the same path, teams can identify failures earlier and reduce manual deployment work.

Keep pipelines declarative where possible, keep credentials out of source control, and make each stage observable.
